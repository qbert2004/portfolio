The Problem

Businesses and NGOs entering emerging markets in Central Asia, Eastern Europe, and the Middle East have no reliable, affordable early-warning system for diplomatic crises, sanctions, or conflict escalation. They rely on expensive consultants or outdated reports.

🎯

Target Market

SME exporters, international NGOs, investment funds with emerging market exposure, and government agencies in Central Asia. Initial focus: Kazakhstan (92%), Uzbekistan (85%), Kyrgyzstan (78%), Tajikistan (70%), Turkmenistan (55%).

💳

Business Model

SaaS subscription: Free for NGOs & universities (5 monitors, weekly digest). $99/mo for SMEs (25 monitors, real-time alerts, AI analysis). $499/mo Enterprise (unlimited, full API, dedicated analyst, SLA).

⚙️

Technology

Data pipeline: 200+ news outlets, UN documents, social media, gov portals, sanction DBs → NLP extraction in 15 languages → Risk Engine (Score 0–100, trend analysis, forecasting) → delivery via Dashboard, REST API, Telegram bot, PDF reports. Alert latency: <5 min. Uptime SLA: 99.2%.

📈

Impact & Projections

Year 1–3 targets: 500+ organizations protected, 94 countries covered, $2.3M ARR by Year 2, 72% crisis detection accuracy. Democratizing geopolitical intelligence — making it accessible to organizations that cannot afford McKinsey or Oxford Analytica.

🛰️

Our Solution

GeoSignal is an AI-powered platform that monitors diplomatic signals, UN resolutions, sanction announcements, and conflict indicators in real-time — delivering risk scores and alerts to decision-makers via a clean dashboard and REST API.
