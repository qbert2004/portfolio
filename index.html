Startup
GeoSignal

GS
Real-time geopolitical risk intelligence — powered by AI
️

The Problem

Businesses and NGOs entering emerging markets in Central Asia, Eastern Europe, and the Middle East have no reliable, affordable early-warning system for diplomatic crises, sanctions, or conflict escalation. They rely on expensive consultants or outdated reports.

Our Solution

GeoSignal is an AI-powered platform that monitors diplomatic signals, UN resolutions, sanction announcements, and conflict indicators in real-time — delivering risk scores and alerts to decision-makers via a clean dashboard and API.

Target Market

SME exporters, international NGOs, investment funds with emerging market exposure, and government agencies in Central Asia. Initial focus: Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, and surrounding regions.

Business Model

SaaS subscription: $99/month for SMEs, $499/month for enterprise. API access for integration with ERP systems. Free tier for NGOs and universities to drive adoption and build data partnerships.

Technology

NLP models trained on diplomatic texts and UN documents. Web scraping of 200+ news sources in 15 languages. Power BI-style dashboards for non-technical users. Integrated with existing risk management tools.

Impact

Democratizing geopolitical intelligence — making it accessible to organizations that cannot afford McKinsey or Oxford Analytica. Helping businesses make ethical, informed decisions about market entry and operations.
